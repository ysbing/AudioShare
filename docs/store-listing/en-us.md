## Description

```
AudioShare streams the sound of your Windows or Mac to an Android phone over USB or Wi-Fi. Nothing is installed on the phone, and no root is required.

The computer captures the audio; the phone plays it.

Getting started
1. Install AudioShare on your PC and start it.
2. Enable USB debugging on the phone, or pair it with ADB over Wi-Fi, then connect it.
3. Press Connect when your phone appears in the list.

Everything playing on your computer now comes out of the phone.

• System audio capture — 48 kHz stereo on Windows (WASAPI loopback) and on macOS 13 or later (ScreenCaptureKit).
• Nothing to install on the phone — ADB and the playback server ship inside AudioShare.
• USB and ADB over Wi-Fi, with automatic reconnection to the last device you used.
• Private by design — nothing is recorded, stored or uploaded.

Requirements
• Windows 10/11 x64 with working ADB drivers, or macOS 13 or later.
• An Android phone or tablet with USB debugging enabled. Android 6.0 or later.

AudioShare speaks 28 languages: English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Italian, Hindi, Arabic, Turkish, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Polish, Thai, Dutch, Swedish, Czech, Hebrew, Ukrainian, Romanian, Greek, Hungarian, Danish and Finnish.
```
